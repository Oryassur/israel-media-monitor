Israel in Western Media

Hourly homepage monitoring of 20 major western outlets — editorial attention and headline framing. Updated …. Methodology

Attention — share of homepage prominence on Israel

Prominence-weighted share of each homepage devoted to Israel-related headlines, averaged equally across selected sources.

Sentiment — framing of Israel in headlines

−2 hostile … +2 sympathetic, prominence-weighted mean of scored headlines. Above zero = sympathetic, below = critical.

Latest Israel-related headlines

Methodology. Every hour, the pipeline captures the headlines on each outlet's homepage. Each headline gets a prominence weight (top-5 slots ×3, upper quarter ×2, rest ×1). Attention is the Israel-related share of a homepage's total weighted headlines, averaged equally across the selected outlets. Sentiment scores each Israel-related headline's framing toward Israel on a fixed rubric (−2 hostile … +2 sympathetic) using a language model; scores are cached per headline and record the model + rubric version. Hours where an outlet failed to respond are excluded, not counted as zero. Headline drill-down covers the most recent 7 days. Source code & data: GitHub.
