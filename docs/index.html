Hourly homepage monitoring of 30 major western outlets — editorial attention and headline framing. Updated …. Methodology

Attention — share of homepage prominence on Israel

Prominence-weighted share of each homepage devoted to Israel-related headlines, averaged equally across selected sources.

Sentiment — framing of Israel in headlines

−2 hostile … +2 sympathetic, prominence-weighted mean of scored headlines. Above zero = sympathetic, below = critical.

Latest Israel-related headlines

Methodology. Every hour, the pipeline captures the headlines on each outlet's homepage. Each headline gets a prominence weight reflecting how top-heavy reader attention is: the lead story ×10, slots 2–5 ×5, 6–10 ×3, 11–20 ×1. Attention is the Israel-related share of the top-20 window's total weight, averaged equally across the selected outlets (only the top-20 window is ingested as of 2026-09-09; stories below it are counted for parser health but not tracked). Sentiment scores each Israel-related headline's framing toward Israel on a fixed rubric (−2 hostile … +2 sympathetic) using a language model; scores are cached per headline and record the model + rubric version. Hours where an outlet failed to respond are excluded, not counted as zero. Political lean (Left / Center / Right) is assigned mechanically from published ratings — AllSides Media Bias Ratings where the outlet is rated (Lean Left/Left → Left, Center → Center, Lean Right/Right → Right), Ad Fontes Media for international outlets they cover, and the widely documented characterization otherwise; the per-outlet basis is noted in config/sources.yaml. Headline drill-down covers the most recent 7 days. On 2026-09-09 the panel expanded from 20 to 30 outlets; Associated Press and El País were retired at the same time (persistent fetch failures) — their history remains in the daily series.
